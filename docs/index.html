What the leaf is doing

The stomatal pore is adjusting to the cues you set. Raise light to open, raise CO₂ or drought to close, and read the sentence trail as the aperture settles.

Why stomata matter

Every green leaf hosts thousands of microscopic mouths. When guard cells swell with turgor, the pore opens and CO₂ floods in for photosynthesis—while water vapor escapes. When they lose turgor, the pore narrows, trading carbon gain for hydraulic safety. Light, CO₂, and the drought hormone ABA are three of the classic levers on that bargain.

How this model works

Stomata Hour blends those cues with fixed teaching weights, then lerps the drawn aperture toward the result each frame. Advanced mode surfaces proxies for phototropin drive, ABA, turgor, soil moisture, and vapor-pressure deficit so you can narrate the same story with more dials visible. Nothing here replaces a laboratory assay.
